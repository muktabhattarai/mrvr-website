A quiet stay beside the Marshyangdi

Our resort sits on the banks of the Marshyangdi River, where the sound of moving water replaces the noise of the road and the hills rise green on every side.

Stay in our cozy cottages, enjoy breakfast by the river, relax by the pool and sauna, or explore the outdoors with riverside walks and rafting. A peaceful riverside escape where comfort, nature, and warm Nepali hospitality come together.
